1. Features
Add home maintenance tasks.
Store repair and maintenance details.
Set task priority and due date.
Assign service providers.
Track estimated and actual expenses.
Update task status.
Search maintenance records.
View pending, completed, and overdue tasks.
Generate expense reports.
